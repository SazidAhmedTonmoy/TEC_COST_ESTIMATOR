TEC ConEst v3.5.9 - small Tender opportunity UI update
22 October 2026

This patch applies over the COMPLETE v3.5.8 application.

CHANGES
1. Approved, Rejected and Expired opportunity deadlines now use normal 12px, regular-weight grey text, without badge padding or background. The same styling applies to their blank/unavailable dates. Pending opportunities retain their existing coloured urgency markers.
2. Approved opportunities no longer show the Reject action on cards OR in their detail panel, even when the deadline has passed. Expired opportunities (not yet approved) keep their Reject option, and awaiting approval can still be rejected in its detail view. Active approved opportunities retain the separate Not quoted outcome.
3. No schema change or opportunity data reset. Release is 3.5.9; database schema remains 32.

INSTALL
1. Back up your live application and databases.
2. If already on complete v3.5.8, use TEC_COST_ESTIMATOR_v3.5.9_CHANGED_FILES.zip. Otherwise use the full package.
3. Extract the ZIP and merge the CONTENTS of TEC_COST_ESTIMATOR into /home/ampecportal/tec-const.ampecportal.com/ preserving subfolders.
4. Upload tenders_web/index.html and ALL new assets in tenders_web/assets. This small release includes precompiled browser assets and DOES NOT require npm on the host. DO NOT upload a node_modules directory.
5. Do not overwrite the live data/ directory, passenger_wsgi.py, .htaccess or your environment configuration.
6. Restart Passenger from cPanel or run in the application root: mkdir -p tmp && touch tmp/restart.txt
7. Refresh the Tender page. Check https://tec-const.ampecportal.com/api/health?check=tenders for release 3.5.9, schema_version 32, and all readiness checks ok.
8. Verify an Approved past-due card: normal-size neutral date, NO Reject button. Verify a genuinely Expired (unapproved) opportunity: Reject remains.
