TEC ConEst v3.5.7 — Opportunity quoting and estimate section switches
Prepared 22 October 2026

WHAT CHANGES

This release adds optional rejection notes, approval through team assignment,
permanent approval after the closing date, a Quoted / Not quoted workflow,
larger coloured deadline labels and estimate section inclusion switches.
Existing themes, mobile layouts and the v3.5.6 Link/import features remain.

OPPORTUNITIES: APPROVAL AND QUOTE OUTCOME

The Reason for rejection and decision-note fields may remain blank. Your
identity, decision and time are still recorded in activity and contributions.

Assigning at least one team automatically moves the opportunity to Approved,
including an expired opportunity. Selected team members and individually
assigned people receive notifications. Team assignment triggers approval;
individual assignments continue to identify and notify their recipients.
Assignment/access management remains limited to the creator or an administrator.
Administrators and eligible team members can also approve an expired entry
directly. Removing a team later does not undo an existing approval.

Once approved, deadline processing leaves the opportunity Approved. Its card
can show a red passed-deadline label while its status stays Approved. Editing
its deadline or other project fields also preserves its approval.

Approved cards and details offer these outcomes:

- QUOTED: enter the Electrical services submission value in AUD. This is the
  amount actually submitted, separate from the project's budget/range and the
  earlier estimated tender value. Select at least one builder already linked
  to the opportunity. Enter an optional quote number and note, choose the
  submitted team, and confirm the submitted date. A blank date uses today in
  the opportunity's region. A zero value is permitted; a blank value is not.
- Saving creates an Open Tender submission for each selected builder under
  the same project. The common submitted value is applied across those
  builders. Project name, region, location, original closing deadline, working
  scope and a source-opportunity link are retained. The screen opens Project
  Chase-up, where you can continue follow-ups and update Tender outcomes.
- The original opportunity remains Approved and displays its quoted amount,
  recorded details and an Open Project Chase-up link. Repeating an identical
  submission request is safe and does not create duplicate records. Later
  quote edits belong in the linked Tender project. If a matching project
  already exists in Projects / Project Chase-up, conversion stops with a
  clear message so the existing submissions are not silently overwritten.
- NOT QUOTED: moves an approved opportunity to Rejected. Its rejection note
  is optional. An already-quoted opportunity is managed through Chase-up,
  where its real submissions now exist.

The original opportunity may contain builders that did not receive the quote.
Selecting only the relevant builders does not remove the others from its
history. Add a builder to the opportunity before quoting if none is recorded.

DEADLINE COLOURS

  Red            Passed deadline, due today, or 1–3 days remaining
  Yellow/amber   4–7 days remaining
  Green          More than 7 days remaining
  Neutral        No deadline, or an unavailable/invalid stored date

The larger badge retains text and the exact closing date. Days are calendar
days, including weekends, in the opportunity's regional timezone. NSW uses
Australia/Sydney; VIC uses Australia/Melbourne. Other configured regions use
their saved timezone. The count changes at local midnight without added API
polling, and the viewer's own device timezone does not change the count.

ESTIMATE SECTION ON/OFF SWITCH

Each section/category heading now has an On/Off switch beside its expand
control. Existing projects and new sections default to On. Off retains all
items, components, quantities, rates and raw category totals, but excludes
that section from the estimate's material, labour, direct cost, markup/quoted
total, labour hours/days and work-type summary totals. The category remains
visible with an Excluded from total label, so it can be reviewed or switched
back on without rebuilding its contents.

The switch requires the same editing access as the estimate. Its change is
recorded, and a stale browser revision cannot silently overwrite newer work.
The setting survives refresh, project copying, revision snapshots/restoration
and Project JSON export/import. New items added within an excluded section
follow that section's setting.

Imported category names with extra spaces are treated as the same displayed
section, while their original stored names remain intact. Public shared
estimates also label excluded categories/items and identify the included total.

Excel exports keep the raw item prices visible. On CONNECTED COST DETAIL,
column P is labelled Include in total. Enter 1 for On or 0 for Off; the cell
displays the corresponding word. Initial values follow the website's section
settings. Workbook totals and summaries use these inclusion flags. These are
item controls inside the exported workbook; workbook edits do not update the
website automatically.

DATABASE UPGRADE AND PRESERVED WORK

The Estimator database upgrades automatically to schema 32. This adds a
project-specific excluded_categories setting whose empty default means all
sections are included. The upgrade uses the existing process lock and verified
pre-upgrade backup. For the reported database location, the catalogue snapshot
is data/upgrade_backups/conest_catalog-before-schema-32.db.

A separate one-time correction checks previously expired opportunities. It
restores Approved only where the current record matches a genuine recorded
approval and a later automatic System expiry, with no later conflicting
decision. It retains the original approver/date and full history, adds an
explicit restoration event, and notifies the relevant recipients. A verified
Tender backup is made before any such correction. Expired records without
that evidence remain available for an explicit approval or team assignment.

Previous legacy-schema repairs remain included. The existing one-time clear
keeps its opportunities-owner-reset-v351 completion key. Completed clears are
not repeated, and retired/deleted records are excluded from restoration. Later
projects, imported work, audit history and contribution records are preserved.

     /home/ampecportal/tec-const.ampecportal.com/

   Keep the existing level of app.py and conest_app/. Complete the upload

     all supplied files under assets/, including new hashed app.*.js

3. Preserve data/, uploads, .htaccess, working passenger_wsgi.py, environment
   settings and the virtual environment. Keep the working Passenger Python:

     /home/ampecportal/tec-conest-py311/bin/python

   A different shell Python does not change Passenger's configured interpreter.

4. After all files are uploaded, use the hosting panel's application Restart
   action, or run this block in the hosting terminal:

bash <<'SH'

5. Open:

     https://tec-const.ampecportal.com/api/health?check=tenders

   Expect success:true, status:"ok", release:"3.5.7", schema_version:32, and

   query is needed to exercise both workspaces. Then hard-refresh the browser:
   Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows/Linux, or Command+Shift+R on macOS.

QUICK ACCEPTANCE CHECK

- Reject an opportunity with a blank reason.
- Assign a team to an expired opportunity and confirm Approved and notification.
- Record a quote with its actual electrical value and selected builders; check
  the opened Chase-up entry, note, source link and Approved/Quoted source card.
- Mark a different approved opportunity Not quoted with a blank note; confirm
  it appears in Rejected.
- Compare 3-, 4-, 7- and 8-day cards and check both desktop and mobile views.
- Switch an estimate section Off. Confirm raw items remain while project cost
  and hours decrease. Refresh, export Excel, and switch On to restore totals.

If startup fails, use the new error reference while signed in as an admin:

  /api/admin/startup-diagnostics?reference=<new-reference>
